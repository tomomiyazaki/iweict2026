The 23rd International Workshop on Emerging ICT

November 4-5, 2026, Sendai, Japan/Hybrid

News

  • Oct 1: The website is available.

Call for Papers

2026 International Workshop on Emerging ICT is an annual conference and aims at a good mutual understanding of educational and research activities of the three universities (Tohoku U, Yeungnam U and DLUT). Undergraduate students and graduate students gather to present their university lives and research topics in the area of electronics and communications. The first conference was held at Yeungnam University in Nov. 2004. This year's conference will be held on-site and online, 4-5 Nov. 2026.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • High-speed communication systems
  • Wireless communication
  • Optical communication
  • Signal processing
  • Image processing
  • Speech and audio processing
  • User interface
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Medical application
  • Intelligent transportation system
  • Intelligent robotics
  • Wireless power transfer
  • Measurement by electromagnetic wave
  • Intelligent hardware
  • Cyber security and hardware security

In addition to ordinary research papers, we welcome survey presentations by undergraduate students. We are planning to organize a special session of short presentations by undergraduate students (Young Researcher's Session).

The International Workshop on Emerging ICT is technically co-sponsored by the ECEI Group, Tohoku University, the Research Center for Electrical Communication, and the Advanced Institute of So-Go-Chi (Convergence Knowledge) Informatics.
